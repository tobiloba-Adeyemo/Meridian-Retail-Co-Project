# Meridian Retail Co. — Retail Sales & Profitability Analysis



Meridian Retail Co. requires a consolidated view of its sales, profitability, customers, products, regional performance, and discounting activities to better understand the factors driving business performance.

The objective of this project is to transform retail transaction data into an interactive Power BI reporting solution that enables users to monitor overall performance, compare regional and category results, evaluate the relationship between discounts and profitability, and understand customer segment behavior.

This project consists of **four interconnected Power BI dashboards**:

1. **Executive Summary**
2. **Regional & Category Performance**
3. **Discount vs Profit Analysis**
4. **Customer Segment Analysis**

Together, these dashboards provide a comprehensive view of Meridian Retail Co.'s performance from executive, geographic, product, profitability, and customer perspectives.

# Dashboard 1: Executive Summary

## Overview

The **Executive Summary Dashboard** provides a high-level overview of Meridian Retail Co.'s overall business performance.

It is designed to help management monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends, and evaluate the company's financial performance.

The dashboard answers important business questions:

- How much revenue is the business generating?
- How profitable is the business?
- How many orders are being processed?
- What is the overall profit margin?
- What is the average discount offered?
- Which months generate the most sales and profit?
- Which regions contribute the most sales?
- Which product categories generate the highest sales?

### Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights five major KPIs:

- **Total Sales:** $470.53K
- **Total Profit:** $62K
- **Total Orders:** 1,038
- **Profit Margin:** 13.10%
- **Average Discount:** 15.56%

These metrics provide an immediate snapshot of the company's financial and operational performance.

Meridian Retail Co. generated approximately **$470.53K in sales across 1,038 orders** resulting in approximately **$61.62K in profit**. The overall profit margin is **13.10%**, while the average discount across transactions is **15.56%**.

### Monthly Sales Trend

The monthly sales trend visualizes changes in sales performance throughout the year.

The strongest months displayed are:

- **November — $76K**
- **December — $75K**
- **September — $65K**

Meanwhile, some of the lower-performing months include:

- **June — $25K**
- **July — $29K**
- **October — $31K**

November and December record the highest sales, indicating stronger sales activity toward the end of the year. Several months in the middle of the year show comparatively lower sales.

The monthly trend provides an overview of sales fluctuations and helps identify periods that may require further investigation.

### Monthly Profit Trend

The monthly profit trend provides additional insight into the company's financial performance.

The strongest profit months displayed are:

- **November — $12K**
- **March — $10K**
- **September — $8K**

January records approximately **-$3K**, indicating a negative monthly profit.

Comparing monthly sales and profit is important because higher sales do not necessarily translate into higher profitability.

The negative profit recorded in January highlights the importance of monitoring profitability alongside revenue.

### Regional Sales Performance

The dashboard compares sales across the company's four regions:

- **East — $156.33K**
- **West — $139.97K**
- **Central — $102.87K**
- **South — $71.36K**

The East generates the highest sales, followed by the West and Central regions. The South records the lowest sales.

This comparison provides management with an overview of the company's geographic sales distribution.

### Sales by Category

The category analysis reveals the following sales distribution:

- **Furniture — $170.52K**
- **Technology — $162.78K**
- **Office Supplies — $137.23K**

Furniture is the largest category by sales, followed closely by Technology.

# Dashboard 3: Discount vs Profit Analysis

## Overview

The **Discount vs Profit Analysis Dashboard** focuses on the relationship between discounting and profitability across product categories.

Discounts are an important part of retail pricing strategies. However, excessive discounting can reduce profit margins if the additional sales generated do not compensate for the reduced selling price.

This dashboard examines sales, profit, average discounts, and profit margins to provide a better understanding of category-level profitability.

It answers important business questions:

- Which categories receive the highest average discounts?
- Which categories generate the most profit?
- Which categories have the highest profit margins?
- How do discount levels differ across categories?
- Which sub-categories contribute the most profit?

### Overall Profitability

The dashboard highlights three major KPIs:

- **Total Profit:** $62K
- **Average Discount:** 15.56%
- **Profit Margin:** 13.10%

These metrics provide an overview of the company's profitability and discounting activities.

### Average Discount by Category

The average discount analysis reveals the following:

- **Furniture — 16.88%**
- **Office Supplies — 16.03%**
- **Technology — 12.69%**

Furniture receives the highest average discount, followed by Office Supplies.

Technology has the lowest average discount among the three categories.

This comparison helps identify differences in discounting practices across product categories.

### Profitability by Category

The dashboard compares total sales, average discounts, total profit, and profit margins across the three major product categories.

| Category | Total Sales | Average Discount | Total Profit | Profit Margin |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Furniture | $170.52K | 16.88% | $3.02K | 1.77% |
| Office Supplies | $137.23K | 16.03% | $25.10K | 18.29% |
| Technology | $162.78K | 12.69% | $33.50K | 20.58% |
| **Total** | **$470.53K** | **15.56%** | **$61.62K** | **13.10%** |

Furniture generates the highest sales but has the lowest profit margin at **1.77%**.

Technology generates slightly lower sales than Furniture but produces approximately **$33.50K in profit**, achieving the highest profit margin at **20.58%**.

Office Supplies generates approximately **$25.10K in profit**, with an **18.29% profit margin**.

The results demonstrate that the category with the highest sales is not necessarily the category with the highest profitability.

### Discount and Profit Relationship

The dashboard highlights an interesting relationship between average discounts and profit margins.

- **Furniture:** Highest average discount and lowest profit margin.
- **Technology:** Lowest average discount and highest profit margin.
- **Office Supplies:** Intermediate average discount and a strong profit margin.

Furniture's average discount of **16.88%** coincides with a profit margin of only **1.77%**.

In comparison, Technology has an average discount of **12.69%** and a profit margin of **20.58%**.

This pattern suggests an opportunity to investigate the relationship between discounting and profitability.

However, discount levels alone do not establish the cause of the differences in profit margins. Product costs, pricing, and product mix may also contribute.

### Top Sub-Categories by Profit

The dashboard also highlights the top-performing sub-categories by profit.

- **Phones — $10.40K**
- **Accessories — $10.20K**
- **Copiers — $9.93K**
- **Binders — $7.60K**

These sub-categories are among the largest contributors to the company's overall profit.

The analysis allows users to identify profitable product groups and compare their contributions to the business.

### Data Cleaning and Transformation

The data cleaning and transformation process was carried out using **Power Query Editor in Power BI**. The objective was to prepare the raw dataset for accurate analysis, establish data consistency and ensure that the data was suitable for building interactive dashboards.

The following steps were performed:

**1. Data Inspection**

The dataset was initially examined in Power Query to understand its structure, identify the available columns, and assess the quality of the data. This provided an overview of the fields required for the sales, profitability, regional and customer analyses.

**2. Data Type Validation**

Column data types were reviewed and assigned appropriately to ensure accurate calculations and aggregations. Numerical fields, such as Sales, Profit and Discount, were checked to ensure they could be used in mathematical calculations, while date and categorical fields were prepared for time-based and segment analysis.

**3. Handling Missing Values**

The dataset was reviewed for missing or null values in relevant columns. This step helped identify potential data quality issues that could affect calculations, visualizations, and the accuracy of the dashboard results.

**4. Duplicate Checks**

Duplicate records were examined to help maintain data integrity and prevent potential inconsistencies in the analysis.

**5. Data Transformation**

Power Query was used to prepare the dataset for analysis by applying the necessary transformations. These included reviewing column structures, standardizing data types, and ensuring that the relevant fields were available for the different dashboard views.

**6. Data Loading**

After cleaning and transformation, the prepared data was loaded into Power BI for data modeling, DAX calculations, and dashboard development.

These preparation steps established a consistent data foundation for the four dashboards and helped ensure that the resulting visualizations and business metrics were based on structured data.
